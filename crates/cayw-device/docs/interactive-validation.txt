Phase 1 behavior retained
InteractiveLimits bounds one pending write and unread stdout. Unique stdin/stdout
endpoints request stop on Drop; cloned ProcessControl can observe/close/wait.
The original supervisor owns Child/group, OS pipes, open cwd and lifetime raw
stdout+stderr output budget. stdin uses nonblocking writes and validates before
copying. Dropping an unfinished write requests stop. stdout backpressure does not
block cancellation, deadlines or stderr draining; stderr counts against budget
and read errors are reported. Cleanup does not wait for a consumer. EOF alone
cannot release a reservation. The join owner publishes outcomes only after a
real join including TLS destruction and resource cleanup. Waiting is repeatable,
supports multiple waiters and can be cancelled without losing cleanup ownership.
The delivered suite also covers literal argv/env, renamed/replaced open cwd,
exact and exceeded output budgets across exchanges, pipe/backpressure errors,
endpoint/control/runner/start-future Drop, supervisor panic, failed supervisor or
join-owner startup, resource refusal, descendant cleanup and Tokio shutdown.
No numeric signal after observed ECHILD or identity loss is permitted; hosts must
not reap Device-owned children themselves. Unknown cleanup retains reservations.

Limits
